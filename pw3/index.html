Trivia

Geography

Which sea does the Nile flow into?

Reveal answer for the Nile question

The Mediterranean Sea. In northern Egypt the river splits into branches that fan across the Nile Delta before reaching the coast.

Science

What is Jupiter’s Great Red Spot?

Reveal answer for the Jupiter question

A giant, long-lived storm in Jupiter’s atmosphere. The reddish oval sits south of the planet’s equator.

History

Which mission first landed people on the Moon?

Reveal answer for the Moon landing question

Apollo 11. Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin landed in July 1969 while Michael Collins remained in lunar orbit.
